D Cast Direct

Cast videos from one device to many others at once. Works with no internet: devices just need the same Wi-Fi (a router without internet is fine) or one phone's hotspot. Scan one QR once (on either device), then play as many videos as you like. Pairing needs internet on both devices; there is also an offline mode.
